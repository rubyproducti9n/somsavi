### 1. Premium Spices & Condiments
SOMSAVI’s blended spices are renowned for their exceptional quality. Each blend is carefully formulated to impart an authentic taste to various Indian dishes, reflecting our deep traditional expertise. Our blends are specifically designed for classical Indian dishes, and we take pride in our traditional approach to cooking, which is completely free from preservatives and artificial flavor enhancers.

* **Seed & Powder Spices:** Red Chilli Powder, Coriander Seed & Powder, Turmeric Powder, Cumin Seed & Powder, Fennel Seed, Mustard Seed, Black Pepper & Powder, Ginger Powder.
* **Whole Spices:** Cardamom, Cloves, Cinnamon, Bay Leaf, Nutmeg.
